FAQs

In case you’re wondering…

Where are the ceremony and the reception taking place?

The ceremony will be held at Capilla de Sta. Rita de Cascia, followed by a reception at Catalina Events Place Rental.

What time should I arrive?

We would love for you to be comfortably seated at Capilla de Sta. Rita de Cascia by 1:00 PM, as the ceremony will begin promptly at 2:30 PM.

We’d appreciate it if you could join us at the ceremony, not just at the reception. It’s the most important part of our wedding, and sharing that moment with you would mean so much.

What happens after the ceremony?

Feel free to head straight to the reception venue and enjoy a delightful cocktail hour at Catalina Events Place Rental.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. We kindly request our guests to arrive in formal or semi-formal attire to make this occasion truly special. Please see the attire guide above for colors and examples.

Can we take photos and post them on social media?

Yes! We would love for you to take photos and share them. However, please refrain from taking photos during the ceremony and do not block our official photographers.

Until what time is the program?

The reception program will wrap up around 9:00 PM, but the celebration doesn’t have to end there. Join us for an after-party with drinks, music, and dancing.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to limited space, we are only able to accommodate guests formally invited on our wedding invitation. Please refer to the number of seats allocated for you. Thank you for understanding.

Can I bring kids?

We adore your children; however, we have decided to keep our wedding celebration adults-only. We truly appreciate your understanding and look forward to celebrating together.

How can I contribute to the celebration?

Your presence is the greatest gift we could ask for. If you wish to give more, a monetary gift would be greatly appreciated. Please message us and we will share the details.

How can I RSVP?

We’d love to hear from you! Kindly RSVP online through the form below, or contact Nicole +63 9XX XXX XXXX or Hanes +63 9XX XXX XXXX. We’ll be waiting for your confirmation until December 15, 2026.

If you have any questions, please feel free to reach out to us via Facebook Messenger.
